Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The web calculator and the Google Sheet are free, with no account. The automated Steam sync is free during the beta; a paid plan comes later, and you decide then whether you want it.

Where are my numbers stored?

In the web calculator, only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. In the Google Sheet, in your own Google account.

Does it work for itch.io, Epic or console sales?

Yes. Type in the revenue you received from any store, and the calculator splits it the same way. The automatic sync starts with Steam.

What do "from first euro" and "after recoupment" mean?

"From first euro" shares are paid on every euro you receive. "After recoupment" shares only start once an advance, or your own costs, has been paid back from revenue. Most rev-share disagreements come from never deciding which one applies.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Dioba calculates from the numbers and contract settings you enter. For contracts and taxes, talk to a lawyer or accountant.
