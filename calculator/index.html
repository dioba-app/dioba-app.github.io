How this revenue share calculator works

Splits

Each person gets a percentage of the revenue your studio actually receives each month, after the store's fee, refunds and VAT. Whatever is left after everyone's share stays with the studio.

Recoupment

If a publisher paid an advance, or you want your own costs back first, enter it as the amount to recoup. "After recoupment" shares only start once revenue has paid it back. "From first euro" shares are paid on every euro from the start.

Caps

A cap is the most a person can be paid in total. The calculator tracks what each person has been paid so far and stops their share when they reach it.

Statements

For each person and month you get a statement: revenue received, the amount their share is based on, what they're owed, what they've been paid so far and how much of their cap is left.

Example: with a €10,000 advance, a composer on 10% from first euro and an artist on 15% after recoupment, a €5,200 month pays the composer €520 and the artist €780 once the advance is paid back. See the full example.
