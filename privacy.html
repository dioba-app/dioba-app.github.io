Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: the calculator keeps your numbers in your own browser, we only see what you type into our forms, there are no analytics or advertising cookies, and we never sell anything about you.

Who we are

Dioba is made by an independent developer in Croatia. If you have any question about your data, write to hello@dioba.app.

The web calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. What you type is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device so it's still there next time. It is never sent to us or anyone else. To delete it, press "Start empty" in the calculator or clear this site's data in your browser.

The Google Sheet

The spreadsheet version is a file you copy into your own Google account or open in your own spreadsheet program. Everything you type into it stays with you. We can't see it.

Forms you fill in

When you ask for the Google Sheet or reserve a beta spot, we collect what you enter in the form, such as your email address, studio name and answers to our questions. The forms run on Google Forms, and the answers are stored in our Google account.

We use this only to:

  • send you the spreadsheet,
  • contact you about the Dioba beta and occasional product updates,
  • understand what game teams need from Dioba.

The legal basis is your consent when you send the form. You can withdraw it and ask us to delete your answers at any time, and every update email says how to stop them.

The automated beta

The beta runs inside your own spreadsheet. Your Steam financial API key and your sales data are stored in that spreadsheet, not on our servers. They are used only to fetch your sales from Steam and calculate statements.

This website

We don't use tracking scripts, tracking pixels or cookies, and the fonts are served from this site rather than a third party. Like any web server, ours keeps short technical logs, including IP addresses, to keep the site secure and working. We also count visits from these logs (for example, how many people opened the calculator and which sites linked to us). That count stays on our server and is never shared. The logs are deleted automatically after about two weeks.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services we use to run Dioba (Google, for the forms), or if the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you, and object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority (AZOP) or the authority in your own country.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
