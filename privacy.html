Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Dioba makes tools that help indie game teams calculate revenue shares. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it. In short: very little, and we never sell it.

The free calculator

The calculator is a Google Sheet that you copy into your own Google account. Everything you type into your copy stays in your account. We can't see it.

Forms you fill in

When you request the calculator or reserve a beta spot, we collect what you enter in the form, such as your email address, studio name and answers to our questions. The forms run on Google Forms, and responses are stored in our Google account.

We use this information only to send you the calculator, contact you about the Dioba beta and occasional product updates, and understand what our users need. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

The Dioba add-on (beta)

The add-on runs inside your own spreadsheet. Your Steam financial API key and your sales data are stored in that spreadsheet, not on our servers. The add-on uses them only to fetch your sales from Steam and calculate statements.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services we use to run Dioba (such as Google), or if the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days.
